# Novelty check — 2026-08-25

## Claim checked

No one-stage relative natural affine matrix interpretation over `N` exists for the Yolcu–Aaronson–Heule eleven-rule mixed binary–ternary system in dimensions 1 or 2, with the two dynamic rules strict and the nine base-conversion rules weak; in dimension 2 coefficients and offsets are unbounded.

## Search result

No publication or preprint stating this exact unbounded dimension-two no-go theorem was found in the sources searchable on 2026-08-25.

The closest work is:

- E. Yolcu, S. Aaronson, M. J. H. Heule, “An Automated Approach to the Collatz Conjecture,” *Journal of Automated Reasoning* 67, 12 (2023), DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1007/s10817-022-09658-8>.
- Author manuscript: <https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~mheule/publications/collatz.pdf>.
- Code: <https://github.com/emreyolcu/rewriting-collatz>.

Yolcu–Aaronson–Heule prove the rewrite-system equivalence, synthesize interpretations for weakened systems, and explicitly identify nonexistence of suitable interpretations for the full system as a future-work direction. They do not state or prove the unbounded two-dimensional natural relative no-go theorem here.

Their paper does contain a different negative theorem: a previously studied **unary** Collatz rewrite encoding has no natural-matrix termination proof even when combined with dependency pairs. That result must be cited and distinguished. It does not settle the mixed-base system `T` treated in the present manuscript.

The searches also checked standard background papers on matrix interpretations and string-rewriting termination. No exact duplicate was found.

## Safe novelty wording

Use:

> To the best of our knowledge, this settles the unbounded two-dimensional natural one-stage relative case of a broader obstruction question raised by Yolcu, Aaronson, and Heule.

Do not use:

> This is the first proof that matrix interpretations cannot prove Collatz.

That broader statement is false: the theorem does not cover higher dimensions, other semirings, dependency pairs, or multi-stage methods.

## Limitation of this check

A literature search cannot prove novelty. Non-indexed manuscripts, unpublished notes, or terminology mismatches may remain. The final novelty determination belongs to expert peer review. This file records the searches and the defensible scope; it is not an independent mathematical audit.
